Bring your own synthetic players and transactions. Every row is checked first; a file with errors loads nothing.

Download Excel template

1. Fill the template

Three sheets: players, transactions, sessions. Example rows are included; replace them with your own. Any tool works, including asking an AI assistant to generate the rows.

  • Bands only: income band and age band. Columns such as gender, ethnicity, exact age or account holder names are rejected.
  • Dates between 2026-04-15 and 2026-10-14. The last 30 days (from 15 Sep) are what gets scored.
  • Use the same card_ref or payout_account_ref on two players to simulate a shared card or account.

2. Upload and check

Tip: "Add" keeps the 490 background players, so the peer check has enough comparable players. "Replace" scores only your file (peer check needs 20+ peers per band).
