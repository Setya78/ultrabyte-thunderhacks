About & AI disclosure

What in this app is computed by rules, what is written by an AI model, and what was built with AI help.

Decided by rules, not by AI

  • Both scores (AML context score and Safer Gambling score): four checks each, fixed formulas, thresholds from the Rules page (versioned, audited).
  • Labels (STR candidate, URGENT): pattern rules traced to FINTRAC indicators and the OLG brief.
  • Suggested next step: a lookup table over bands and labels. The rule is shown next to every suggestion.
  • Version 1.0 (separate console at /v1): an illustrative reconstruction of today's routine, built by the team for comparison. A single threshold rule, separate look-up screens, notes kept outside the system. Not OLG's system.

No machine learning model is involved in any score, label, ranking or suggestion.

Written by AI (optional, labelled)

Case summary on a player page, only when AI summary is on and an operator clicks "Generate AI summary".

  • Model and provider: Anthropic Claude ({{ llm_model }}), called through an n8n workflow hosted by the team.
  • What is sent: the computed reasons only: scores, bands, check reasons, labels, suggestion, rule version. No raw transactions, no names, no identifiers beyond the synthetic player ID.
  • What it may do: reword those reasons into three sentences. It cannot change a score, label or suggestion.
  • Safeguards: every number in the reply must appear in the input, otherwise the rule text is shown; the summary is cached per score snapshot and written to the audit trail with the model name and the requesting operator.
  • Status now: AI summary is {{ 'on' if llm_enabled else 'off' }}.

How this app was built

Built during ThunderHacks II (14-16 October 2026) by team UltraByte Solutions. AI coding assistants (Anthropic Claude) helped write code, the synthetic data generator and documentation; design decisions were made by the team and every change is in the commit history and BUILD-LOG. The team logo is an AI-generated image (Google Gemini). All data is synthetic; no real players or transactions.

Full disclosure per component is in the README of the repository.
